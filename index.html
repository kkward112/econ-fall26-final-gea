UN Sustainable Development Goal 7

Affordable and clean energy for everyone

Electricity lights classrooms, keeps vaccines cold and powers small businesses. Yet hundreds of millions of people still live without it. This site explains why Goal 7 matters, how engineers are solving the problem, and what it means for the economy.
