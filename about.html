Why we chose this goal

When a clinic has no reliable power, it cannot refrigerate vaccines. When a student has no light after sunset, homework stops. We wanted to understand why, in a world with abundant sunshine and wind, so many people still lack basic energy — and what practical, affordable fixes already exist.

How we worked

Each member researched one area using reports from the United Nations, the International Energy Agency (IEA), the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA), the World Bank and the World Health Organization. All figures on this site link to the Sources page.
