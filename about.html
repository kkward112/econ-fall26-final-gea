About us

We’re SolarPunk

Four electrical engineering students at the University of South Florida. We give you an honest picture of clean energy: what it can save, what it costs, and when it may not be the right fit.

Why this matters now

Electricity is becoming one of the fastest-rising costs in American households. Demand is climbing as the growth of artificial intelligence drives the construction of data centers. The energy choices we make today also shape the communities we will live in tomorrow. As Florida homeowners weigh whether clean energy like solar makes sense for them, they deserve information that is accurate and free of a sales pitch.

Why you can trust us

One of our main learning goals is understanding energy systems: how electricity is generated, delivered and used. Our team has tested the same class of enterprise servers that fill today’s data centers, so we have seen firsthand the demand that is driving up energy prices. We have also completed industry internships directly connected to power systems and how they are used every day. In every lab and project, we have learned to test ideas against evidence instead of accepting the first answer.

Technical knowledge alone doesn’t make engineers trustworthy communicators. Whether we were teaching robotics to campers with no technical background or helping everyday customers through a small business, we learned to meet people where they are. As leaders in engineering student organizations, we have also coordinated classmates, recruiters and industry partners toward shared goals.

We have nothing to sell you

SolarPunk earns nothing from your decision. As Floridians who have lived through major hurricanes, we know how much a reliable source of power matters at home. We want you to make the choice that is best for your community.
